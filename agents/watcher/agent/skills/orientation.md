# Orient and hold

Observe the following historical sequence. An orientation may change after a step completes; this does not undo the past observation.

- pick-up: Pick up the tall red Pringles tube.
- horizontal-first: Hold the tube horizontally for the first time.
- vertical: Hold the tube vertically after its first horizontal position.
- horizontal-again: Hold the tube horizontally again after the vertical position. Must be a later distinct observation.
- second-can: Pick up a second, smaller beverage can while also holding the Pringles tube.
