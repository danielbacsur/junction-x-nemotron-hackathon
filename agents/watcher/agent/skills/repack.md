# Unpack and repack

Observe these historical actions in order. The bag's printed Arduino logo is not the Arduino object.

- remove-pringles: Take the tall red Pringles tube out of the tote bag.
- remove-arduino: Take the rectangular Arduino package or box out of the tote bag. Do not verify if only a hand or bag logo is visible.
- replace-pringles: Put the Pringles tube back into the tote bag after taking the Arduino package out.
